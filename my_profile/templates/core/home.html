Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Send us a message below.

Who will work on my project?

One of our three team members leads your project based on the service you need. For larger projects, we work on it together.

How do payments work?

Most projects are 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. Larger projects are split into milestones.

How long will my project take?

Small projects take about a week. Larger builds take 2 to 4 weeks or more, depending on scope. We confirm the timeline in the proposal.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work remotely with clients anywhere and can adjust meeting times to your time zone.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Each package includes revision rounds and a support window. Extra work after that is quoted separately.

Do I own the final work?

Yes. After final payment, you own the deliverables and source files.
